# Worked example (made up)

A made-up home bakery, to show the shape of the work. The full reply shape for a matrix is in `content-matrix.md`, section 5. The business, the accounts and the scores are invented for this example only. With a real owner, every source and score comes from their own files.

**The owner:** Hilltop Bakes, custom cookies and birthday boxes, sells through Instagram DMs. Films 2 videos a week on Mondays, sometimes on camera. No comment-to-DM tool. Has `research-notes.md` from 3 days ago. No results yet.

## Growing one idea

The owner has told us one thing about storage: "never put them in the fridge". The web search links below are made up for this example (example.com is not a real source).

```markdown
### I19: How to keep cookies fresh from a Thursday pick-up to a Saturday party
What everyone else posts about this: "store in an airtight container"
Your own take: "never put them in the fridge"

Topic check (2026-10-31, quick web search. Not facts until you check them.)
| # | Kind | What we found | Link | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | People get it wrong | Many people think iced cookies must go in the fridge | https://example.com/storing-iced-cookies | check this is true for you |
| F2 | People argue about | Some say freeze cookies for a party, others say freezing ruins the icing | https://example.com/forum/freeze-decorated-cookies | check this is true for you |

| Angle | Type | One line | Hook direction | Best format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I19a | The mistake | The fridge dries them out (your own take) | open on a cookie going into the fridge, then stop it | Talking head |
| I19b | The surprise | Freeze or not for a party: what you actually do (F2) | hold up two boxes, ask which one was frozen | Talking head |
| I19c | The how-to | Pick up Thursday, party Saturday: [your steps for keeping them fresh] | show the box, the bag, the cupboard | Voice over clips |
| I19d | The story | [your real story: a time an order had to last the weekend] | open on the moment you found out | Talking head |
| I19e | The myth | "Iced cookies have to go in the fridge" (F1) | say the myth out loud, then shake your head | Text on screen only |
```

The mistake angle is the owner's own words, so it needs no link. The surprise and myth angles lean on findings, so they name them, and both findings stay marked "check this is true for you" until the owner answers. The how-to and story angles have blanks because the owner hasn't told us their steps or that story yet. We never make them up. The blanks and one "are F1 and F2 true for you?" question go into "Open questions for the owner" in `team-status.md` (or a "Questions for you" list in the reply), so the owner gets them all in one message.
