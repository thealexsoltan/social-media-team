# Shared words for hook types and formats

Every teammate uses exactly these words, so the plan, the scripts, the research and the results all line up and can be counted. If nothing fits, use `Other` and describe it.

## Hook type (how the post opens)

These match the kinds of hook in `four-hooks.md`, plus two that small businesses post a lot.

| Hook type | What it looks like | Made-up example |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | Tells the viewer about a mistake they might be making | "Stop rinsing your paintbrush in the sink" |
| Surprising fact | Something the viewer believes that isn't true, or a number that shocks | "This much hair came out of one cat" |
| Before and after | Shows the change, then explains it | the messy room, then the tidy room |
| Question | Opens with a question | "Did you know you should..." / "What is...?" |
| Story start | Drops the viewer into a moment | "She walked in holding a broken heel..." |
| List | "3 signs...", "5 things..." | "3 signs your bike chain is worn out" |
| Challenge | "Bet you didn't know..." | "Bet you can't guess what this tool does" |
| How-to | Straight into showing how to do something | "Here's how to fix a wobbly table leg" |
| Update | News about the business: hours, openings, offers, a new van | "We're open Saturdays now" |
| Other | Anything else | |

When a post could be two types, pick the one the **first 2 seconds** lean on. A before and after with a question on screen is still `Before and after` if the first frame shows the change.

## Format (what the video or post looks like)

| Format | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Talking head | The owner talking to camera most of the time |
| Hands at work | The job being done, little or no face |
| Voice over clips | The owner's voice over footage of something else |
| Text on screen only | No talking, the words on screen carry it |
| Slideshow | A string of photos or short clips, often to music |
| Carousel | Swipeable photo or text slides |
| Photo | One picture |
| Screen recording | A phone or computer screen |
| Text post | Words only, no picture (Facebook, LinkedIn, Threads) |
| Story | A short-lived story post |
| Other | Anything else |

"Before and after" is a way to open (a hook type), not a format. A before and after filmed by hand is format `Hands at work`, hook type `Before and after`.
