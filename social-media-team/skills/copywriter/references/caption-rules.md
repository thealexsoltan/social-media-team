# Caption rules

A caption is the text under a video or photo. For a video it does three jobs:
1. Adds to the video (a detail, the steps, the "why"). It doesn't just repeat it.
2. Gives people a reason to comment, save or share.
3. Says what to do next.

## Calls to action (the same rule the whole team follows)

| Owner has... | Use |
|---|---|
| A comment-to-DM tool | "Comment {WORD} and I'll send you {thing}". Pick one short, easy word in capitals |
| No DM tool | "Link in bio", "Call/text {number}", "Save this for later", "Follow for more" |

The profile's main goal picks among these: bookings, leads or sales use the booking route; "Save this" or "Follow" only when the goal is followers or reach.
| Bookings goal | The booking route from `business-profile.md` |

One call to action per caption. Never two.

## Per platform

- **Instagram**: the first line shows before "more", so lead with it. 3 to 5 hashtags at most, at the end, only if the owner uses them.
- **TikTok**: short. One or two lines is normal. The video does the talking.
- **Facebook**: can be a little longer and chattier. Local details work well (town names, weather, local events).
- **LinkedIn**: no slang the owner wouldn't use at work. Short lines, a clear point, a question at the end.
- **YouTube Shorts**: the title matters most. Short, clear, keyword first.

## A caption pack

When the Copywriter writes captions for one video, hand back one caption per platform the owner uses (from `business-profile.md`), each in its own code block, labelled with the platform.

No `word:` headings inside a caption ("the fix:", "what to do:", "step 1:"). Write it as a normal sentence or a plain list.
