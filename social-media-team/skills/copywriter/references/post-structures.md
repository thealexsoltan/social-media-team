# Post structures that work

Pick the structure that fits the idea. Never show the owner the structure's name inside the post itself.

| Structure | Shape | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem, fix, proof** | Name a problem the customer has > the fix > a real example (only if the owner gave one) | Tips, services |
| **Before and after** | What it looked like > what changed > what we did | Visual businesses: grooming, cleaning, trades, fitness |
| **Myth buster** | "Most people think X" > why it's wrong > what to do instead | Experts, coaches, trades |
| **Quick list** | "3 signs / 4 mistakes / 5 things" > short points > what to do | Saves and shares |
| **Story** | A moment > what happened > the lesson for the reader | Trust, personality |
| **Behind the scenes** | What a normal day or job looks like | Local businesses, makers |
| **Answer a question** | "A customer asked me..." > the honest answer | Anyone. The owner's FAQ is a gold mine |
| **Offer** | Who it's for > what you get > how to book or buy > any real deadline | Selling, max 1 in 4 posts (what counts as an offer post: `files-layout.md`) |

## Rules for any structure

- The first line does the most work. It shows in the feed before "more". Make it about the reader.
- One idea per post.
- Short paragraphs. White space.
- A clear next step at the end, using the CTA rules in `caption-rules.md`.
- Proof has to be real. If there's no real example, use a structure that doesn't need one.
- Deadlines and "only 2 spots left" only when the owner says it's true.
