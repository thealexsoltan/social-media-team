# Platform notes

Platforms change their rules often. These notes stick to things that have held steady. If the owner tells you something changed, believe them and add it to `learned-rules.md`.

| Platform | Main format | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Reels (vertical video), carousels (swipe posts), photos | Reels reach new people. Carousels get saved. The caption's first line shows before "more" |
| TikTok | Vertical video | Fast start matters most. Text on screen is normal. Trends come and go fast |
| YouTube Shorts | Vertical video | The title is searchable, so put the plain topic in it |
| Facebook | Video, photos, text posts | Older, local audiences. Local details work well |
| LinkedIn | Text posts, documents, video | Work topics only. Short lines, a clear point |

## Video basics (all vertical platforms)

- Shoot vertical (9:16).
- Keep words and faces away from the very top and bottom, where the app's buttons and captions sit.
- Burn in text for the first few seconds. Many people watch with the sound off.
- Good light beats a good camera. Face a window.

## Posting one video in several places

The same video can go on Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Change the caption for each one (see `caption-rules.md`). Don't post a video with another app's logo on it.
