# Writing script templates from winning reels

A template is a winning reel's script with every specific taken out, so the owner can say the same kind of thing about their own business. It keeps the skeleton: the order of the lines, the connector words, the rhythm. It's a fill-in-the-blank script, not a description of one.

The file is `my-content-team/script-templates.md` (layout in `files-layout.md`). The Scriptwriter reads it and fills the blanks with the owner's facts.

## Which reels

One template per **winning** reel you broke down (score 2.0 or more, or the best reels of an account where the whole account is winning). Never from the contrast posts. Best score first. Number them 1, 2, 3 in the order you add them, and never renumber: add new ones at the end.

A reel with `[no speech]`: its template is the words on screen, in order, as lines (`Text: "..."`), plus the first frame in one plain line. If it had no words at all, skip it and say so.

## How to turn a transcript into a template

Work from the breakdown's spoken lines (section 0) and its words on screen (section 2).

1. **Keep every line, in order**, as one line each. Split long sentences where the speaker breathes.
2. **Keep the connector words**: "if you're", "here's what", "but", "so", "and that's why", "the problem?". They carry the rhythm.
3. **Turn every specific into a `[blank]`** with a short plain label of what goes there:
   - the topic, the claim, the advice: `[your claim]`, `[the mistake people make]`
   - numbers, prices, dates, ages, amounts: `[number]`, `[how long it took]`
   - names, places, brands, products, people: `[a customer]`, `[your town]`, `[the tool you use]`
   - the story: `[what happened, one line]`
   - their catchphrases, sign-offs and calls to action: `[your sign-off]`, `[your call to action]`
4. **No beat labels.** Never write "Hook:", "Beat 2", "Payoff". Just the lines. The owner should be able to read it out with their facts in it.
5. **Read it with the blanks filled in for a different business** (a plumber, a baker). If it doesn't make sense, a specific is still in there: blank it.
6. **Check for leftovers:** no handle, name, brand, product, place, number or catchphrase from the creator may stay in a template line. Their words stay only in the study folder.
7. A long reel (over about 60 seconds): keep the whole skeleton, but where the speaker lists several examples in a row, keep the first and write `[more examples, the same way]`.

## Two versions of every template: short and full

Winning reels often run 60 to 120 seconds, but most of the owner's videos should be 20 to 45 seconds. So every template has two blocks:

- **Short version (the default):** the hook line or lines, the 3 strongest parts of the middle (the lines that carry the reveal, the proof or the turn), and the call to action. About 20 to 45 seconds out loud (50 to 110 words with the blanks filled, about 2.5 words a second). Same lines as the full one, in the same order, just fewer of them: never new lines.
- **Full version:** the whole skeleton from steps 1 to 7, for when the owner asks for a long video.

A reel that's already under about 45 seconds: the short version is the whole thing; write "Short and full are the same." instead of a second block.

Made-up example. A reel says: "If you've been cleaning your oven with soap, stop. That's why it smells. Do this instead: baking soda, vinegar, twenty minutes. Follow for more kitchen tricks."

The template (already short, so short and full are the same):
```
If you've been [doing a common thing], stop.
That's why [the problem it causes].
Do this instead: [your fix, in 2 or 3 short steps].
[your call to action]
```

## The tags

Above each template, from the breakdown:
- Hook type and format: the `formats.md` words from the breakdown's front matter.
- Length: the reel's length, and about how many seconds each version runs ("short: about 35 seconds, full: about 95").
- From: the account and the score with its sum, plus the breakdown's file name, so anyone can check where it came from. This line is the only place the account is named.
- Best for: which kind of post it suits, in plain words ("a myth you hear from customers", "a quick tip with 3 steps", "a story from a job"). If the reel only works on camera, say "needs you on camera".

## Before you save

- Every template has a short version (20 to 45 seconds) and a full one, or says they're the same.
- Every template line has the blanks, no leftovers (step 6).
- No long or short dashes as punctuation.
- The owner's never-say words aren't in any template line (check `business-profile.md` and `learned-rules.md`).
- Update "Last updated" and the "Made from" line.
