# Picking the 10 accounts to learn from (the 5-3-2 mix)

We don't copy competitors. We study what's working for them so the owner can make their own version.

## The mix

| How many | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **5** | Same niche, ideally a bit bigger than the owner (other mobile groomers, other career coaches) | Same customers, so their winners are the closest guide |
| **3** | Broader niche (dog care in general, career advice in general) | Bigger audience, more tested ideas to borrow |
| **2** | Great content makers in any field | They teach format: hooks, pacing, editing, thumbnails |

Local businesses: same-niche accounts can be in other towns or countries. Nobody in another city is a real competitor.

## What makes an account worth keeping

Pick on **steady winners**, not size:
- Posts regularly (at least weekly) in the last 2 months.
- At least 2 posts in the last 15 with an outlier score of 2.0 or more (see `outlier-score.md`), **or** the whole account is winning (the whole-account check in `outlier-score.md`: normal at least 2 times their followers, or at least 2 times the middle normal of the other accounts we studied).
- Makes the kind of content the owner could make (if the owner won't show their face, pick some faceless accounts).

Drop accounts that only went viral once a long time ago, buy views, or post content the owner would never make.

## Qualifying an account from a screenshot

Ask for a screenshot of the account's Reels tab (Instagram) or profile grid (TikTok), where view counts show. Then:
1. Read every view count you can see. Say how many you could read.
2. Find the normal (the middle number) and the winners.
3. Run the whole-account check (`outlier-score.md`). The follower count is in the grid's header.
4. Verdict: **Keep**, **Maybe** or **Skip**, with one line why. Before you write it, check it against the rules above: Keep needs 2 or more winners in the last 15, or a whole account that's winning. An account that passes only the whole-account check gets **Keep (whole account winning)**. An account on the list whose grid we haven't seen yet is **Not checked**.
