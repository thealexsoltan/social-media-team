# Filming checklist

Hand this to the owner when they ask how to film, the first time they get a card, or with a shoot plan. Show the short version under a card. Show the full version when they ask.

## Short version (paste under a card)

```
Before you post:
1. Something moves and something makes a sound in the very first moment.
2. Film the hook 3 times. Keep the best one.
3. Put the text hook on screen from the first frame.
4. Cut every pause, even tiny ones.
5. End on the payoff, then the call to action. Nothing after.
6. Done beats perfect. Post it.
```

## Full version

### Set up (about 2 minutes, rough guess)
- Hold or prop the phone upright (vertical).
- Face a window or a bright light. Good light matters more than a good phone.
- Wipe the lens.
- Keep your face and any text away from the very top and very bottom of the screen. The app's buttons and captions sit there.
- Quiet room, or a sound that belongs in the video (water running, clippers buzzing, tools).

### The first 2 seconds
- Start with movement: walk into frame, lift the thing up to the camera, swing the phone up, show the before.
- Start with sound: you already talking, a splash, a snap, a tool starting. No silent start.
- Movement is for the hook. After that, keep the camera steady.
- No "hey guys" and no "so today I want to talk about". Start on the first real line.

### Filming
- Film the hook at least 3 times, a little differently each time.
- Film each beat as its own short clip. Easier to cut.
- Sounds stiff reading it word for word? Read the card twice, then say it your way. Keep the hook word for word. Or ask the Scriptwriter for the freestyle version.
- Grab the [SHOW] shots on the card while you're there: close-ups, hands working, the before, the after.
- For a before and after, film both from the same spot and angle.
- No talking? Hands, close-ups and the result carry it. Film more shots than you think you need.
- Film the call to action twice. Say it slowly and clearly.

### Editing (on your phone)
- Cut every pause and every "um", even short ones. Tight feels faster and keeps people watching.
- Cut anything that isn't useful to the viewer, like "I'm filming this because".
- Put the text hook on screen from the first frame. You can leave it up the whole video so people always know what it's about.
- Add captions. Lots of people watch with the sound off.
- End on the payoff, then the call to action. Cut the goodbye.
- Watch it once with the sound off. Does it still make sense?

### Saving and posting
- Save the video with the date and short name from the card (for example `2026-11-03-nail-trims`), so you can match it to the card and the results later.
- One video can go on Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Don't post a video with another app's logo on it.
- Spotted a small flaw? Post it anyway. Speed beats polish.
